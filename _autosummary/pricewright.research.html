pricewright.research¶

Market research as an input: comparables packets, normalised and compared.

Research is kept apart from the model on purpose. A packet is a YAML (or JSON) list of comparable prices, gathered by an agent or supplied by the user, in the schema below; it can be swapped or refreshed without touching any offering. The model produces a cost-based price; a packet only tells you where that price sits in the market (position()).

Schema (one record):

id, specialty, offering, provider, region, price | price_low + price_high,
currency, unit, contact_hours, duration_s, group_size, delivery,
provider_tier, evidence (fetched | secondary | snippet | inferred), date, url, notes

pricewright.research.position(value, comparables, *, metric='per_person_contact_hour', fx=None, currency='EUR')[source]¶

Locate value (already in metric units and currency) among comparables.

fx maps a currency to its value in currency (e.g. {"USD": 0.87} when currency='EUR'). A comparable in a currency missing from fx is skipped rather than silently treated as equal.

>>> cs = [Comparable(str(i), "training", "x", price=p, unit="per_person",
...                  contact_hours=1, evidence="fetched") for i, p in enumerate([40, 50, 60, 70, 80])]
>>> position(65, cs).verdict, position(65, cs).median
('within market', 60.0)
