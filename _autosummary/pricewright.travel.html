pricewright.travel

pricewright.travel¶

Travel as explicit variables: time away, nights, subsistence, transport.

An on-site engagement costs the provider three things an online one does not: time (travel hours that could have been billed elsewhere), nights away (when the schedule cannot be done in a day), and expenses (transport, hotel, meals). Industry practice quotes them separately from the professional fee, so a client in the same city and a client a flight away see the same fee and a different travel line. This module turns a Trip into that line, under a TravelPolicy that holds the provider’s terms.

Two ways of billing travel time are in common use, and both are offered:

  • "hourly": door-to-door hours beyond a free allowance each way, billed at a fraction of the hourly rate (the default fraction is 0.5);

  • "day_blocks": each travel leg rounded up to half or full days, billed at a fraction of the day rate (speakers and crews often quote a “travel day”).

Expenses are passed through at cost by default (expense_markup=0), estimated up front from the policy’s hotel cap and per diem, and invoiced against receipts.
